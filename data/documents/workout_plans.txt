FITMIND AI — WORKOUT PLANS
===========================

BEGINNER FULL-BODY PROGRAM (3 DAYS/WEEK)
------------------------------------------
Suitable for: those new to resistance training or returning after a break of 3+ months.
Frequency: Monday / Wednesday / Friday (or any 3 non-consecutive days).
Sets & Reps: 3 sets x 10–12 reps per exercise. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.

Day A:
  - Goblet Squat
  - Push-Up (or Bench Press)
  - Seated Cable Row (or Dumbbell Row)
  - Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
  - Plank (3 x 30 seconds)

Day B (alternate with Day A):
  - Leg Press
  - Incline Dumbbell Press
  - Lat Pulldown
  - Dumbbell Lunges
  - Bicycle Crunches (3 x 15 reps per side)

Progression: Add 2.5 kg to lower body lifts and 1.25 kg to upper body lifts each week when all 3 sets are completed cleanly.

INTERMEDIATE UPPER/LOWER SPLIT (4 DAYS/WEEK)
----------------------------------------------
Suitable for: those with 6–12 months consistent training.
Frequency: Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri. Wednesday and weekends are rest or light cardio.

Upper A (Monday):
  - Barbell Bench Press: 4 x 6–8
  - Barbell Row: 4 x 6–8
  - Overhead Press: 3 x 8–10
  - Cable Row: 3 x 10–12
  - Tricep Pushdown: 3 x 12–15
  - Dumbbell Curl: 3 x 12–15

Lower A (Tuesday):
  - Back Squat: 4 x 6–8
  - Romanian Deadlift: 3 x 8–10
  - Leg Press: 3 x 10–12
  - Leg Curl: 3 x 12–15
  - Calf Raise: 4 x 15

Upper B (Thursday): Same as Upper A with 5–10% volume variation (drop sets or higher reps).
Lower B (Friday): Same as Lower A. Add Deadlift 3 x 4–6 as primary movement.

ADVANCED PPL (PUSH / PULL / LEGS — 6 DAYS/WEEK)
-------------------------------------------------
Suitable for: 2+ years consistent training; strong recovery capacity.
Push: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps
Pull: Back, Biceps, Rear Delts
Legs: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves

Run the cycle twice per week (Push / Pull / Legs / Push / Pull / Legs / Rest).

Push Day:
  - Barbell Bench Press: 4 x 5 (heavy)
  - Incline DB Press: 3 x 10–12
  - Cable Flye: 3 x 15
  - Overhead Press: 3 x 8–10
  - Lateral Raise: 4 x 15–20
  - Skull Crushers: 3 x 12

Pull Day:
  - Weighted Pull-Up: 4 x 6–8
  - Barbell Row: 4 x 6–8
  - Seated Cable Row: 3 x 12
  - Face Pull: 3 x 20
  - Incline Dumbbell Curl: 3 x 12
  - Hammer Curl: 3 x 15

Legs Day:
  - Back Squat: 4 x 5 (heavy)
  - Leg Press: 3 x 15
  - Bulgarian Split Squat: 3 x 10 each leg
  - Leg Curl: 4 x 12
  - Calf Raise: 5 x 20

CARDIO GUIDELINES
------------------
LISS (Low-Intensity Steady State): 30–60 min at 60–70% max heart rate. Examples: brisk walking, cycling. Best for fat loss with minimal muscle interference.
HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training): 20–30 min, 30–40 seconds max effort / 60–90 seconds rest x 8–12 rounds. Maximises calorie burn and cardiovascular adaptation in minimal time. Limit to 2–3 sessions per week; excess HIIT impairs recovery.
Zone 2 Training: Moderate intensity (can hold a conversation). Builds aerobic base and mitochondrial density. 3–5 hours/week is ideal for general health and endurance athletes.
