# FitMind AI — Sample Meal Plans

## Plan A: Fat Loss (approx. 1,800 kcal, 160g protein)

**Breakfast (~450 kcal)**
- 4 egg whites + 2 whole eggs scrambled (24g protein)
- 80g rolled oats with water, topped with 100g blueberries
- Black coffee or green tea

**Mid-Morning Snack (~200 kcal)**
- 200g low-fat Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds

**Lunch (~500 kcal)**
- 150g grilled chicken breast (35g protein)
- 150g cooked brown rice
- Large mixed salad (spinach, cucumber, tomato, capsicum) with 1 tsp olive oil and lemon

**Afternoon Snack (~150 kcal)**
- 1 medium apple
- 20g almonds

**Dinner (~500 kcal)**
- 150g baked salmon (30g protein)
- 200g roasted sweet potato
- 200g steamed broccoli and green beans

**Evening (optional, ~100 kcal)**
- 150g cottage cheese before bed (slow-release casein protein)

## Plan B: Muscle Gain / Bulk (approx. 3,000 kcal, 200g protein)

**Breakfast (~650 kcal)**

**Mid-Morning Snack (~350 kcal)**

- 2 scoops whey protein, 500ml whole milk, 1 large banana

**Dinner (~700 kcal)**
- 200g chicken thighs (skin-on for calories)
- 250g roasted potatoes with olive oil
- 200g mixed roasted vegetables

**Before Bed (~150 kcal)**
- 200g cottage cheese + 1 tsp honey

## Vegetarian / Plant-Based Swaps

| Animal source | Plant-based swap | Protein difference |
|---|---|---|
| 150g chicken breast (35g protein) | 200g firm tofu + 30g hemp seeds | ~35g protein |
| 1 scoop whey (25g protein) | 1 scoop pea protein isolate | ~23–25g protein |
| 150g salmon (30g protein) | 180g tempeh + 1 tsp flaxseed oil | ~31g protein |
| 200g Greek yogurt (18g protein) | 200g soy yogurt + 2 tbsp nutritional yeast | ~18g protein |
| 2 eggs (12g protein) | 100g chickpeas + 1 tbsp tahini | ~11g protein |

**Note:** Plant proteins often have lower leucine content and digestibility. Aim for 10–15% more total protein when following a plant-based plan to compensate.
