# JARVIS Combat Strategy & Tactical Notes
# Chunking note: RECURSIVE / STRUCTURE-AWARE chunks. These are procedures with ordered
# steps - a generic fixed-size splitter would risk cutting a step mid-sentence and
# silently dropping a threshold value, exactly like the course's ingestion mistake story.

Procedure: Drone Swarm Engagement (validated during the Mark 42 drone incident)
Step 1: Divert non-essential power (HUD cosmetic effects, cabin climate control) to weapons systems.
Step 2: Prioritize target selection by proximity to civilians first, then by drone weapon class.
Step 3: If drone count exceeds 12 simultaneous contacts, request backup rather than engaging solo.
Step 4: Maintain a minimum 15% power reserve at all times for emergency thruster burst.
Step 5: If power core output drops below 20% mid-engagement, disengage and return to base - do not
attempt to finish the fight on reserve power.

Procedure: Single High-Threat Target (validated against the Mandarin incident debrief)
Step 1: Establish target's power source and attack pattern before closing distance.
Step 2: Keep a minimum 3-second thruster reserve for evasive maneuvering at all times.
Step 3: If target demonstrates an unknown attack capability, retreat to observation range and
reassess rather than continuing to press the engagement blind.
Step 4: Loop in any nearby allied suits (FRIDAY-monitored assets) before escalating to a
high-risk maneuver.

Procedure: Emergency Extraction (Tony incapacitated or suit critically damaged)
Step 1: Immediately switch to autopilot return-to-base, overriding manual control.
Step 2: Broadcast a distress signal on the private Stark frequency and to any allied suit in range.
Step 3: If altitude is above 500 meters and thruster failure is detected, deploy the emergency
parachute system before attempting any other recovery action.
