Stark Industries - JARVIS Protocol Manual

Protocol: House Party

Summons every suit currently in the vicinity to converge on Tony's location and deploy in remote-piloted mode, without requiring a human pilot inside any individual unit.

  • Only authorized above clearance level 4 (see allies directory).
  • Capped at 6 remotely piloted suits per operator to keep JARVIS's response latency within combat-safe limits.

Protocol: Clean Slate

Permanently erases all data associated with the Iron Man identity across Stark Industries servers and personal devices, including suit blueprints and JARVIS's own combat-strategy knowledge base.

  • Irreversible - requires Tony's explicit, repeated verbal confirmation before execution begins.
  • JARVIS will refuse to execute this protocol if issued under duress indicators (elevated heart rate plus a third party present) without a secondary confirmation.

Protocol: Veronica

Deploys the orbital satellite-linked Hulkbuster armor to Tony's location. Reserved for threats that exceed the tactical envelope of any single standard Mark suit.

  • Requires a two-step authorization: Tony's command plus a secondary confirmation from either Pepper or Rhodey.
  • Deployment time from command to arrival is weather- and orbital-position-dependent - JARVIS must state the current estimate before confirming activation.
